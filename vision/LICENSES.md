# Piece templates — credits and licences

`templates.png` holds small (64×64) renders of the piece sets below. "Read from image" compares
board squares against them. It is built by `tools/make_templates.py`; the first seven sets come
from `pieces/` (see [pieces/README.md](../pieces/README.md)), the rest from
[lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece), with the
licences listed in lila's [COPYING.md](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md).
